## Interpretation and Limitations

Automated review candidates are model-generated suggestions and do not independently establish a PPE violation or safety hazard.

Manual visual findings apply only to the reviewed images. The absence of an automated review candidate does not establish that an image contains no safety concern.

The ladder observation is documented separately from automated detections. No conclusion about harness use is made because it was not confirmed during the review.
